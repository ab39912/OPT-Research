# Source Script

**Provenance:** adapted from the coach-advisory narrative in Task 5
(`Task_05_Descriptive_Stats`), which was itself backed by ground-truth analysis
of the 2025 Syracuse Women's Lacrosse season. Every number below traces to that
verified analysis, so the synthetic voice is reciting a real analytical argument,
not filler.

**Length:** ~260 words, roughly 90-100 seconds at a natural pace.

**Note on the disclosure lines:** the opening and closing lines are the spoken
synthetic-media disclosure required by the task. Keep them in every generated
version. If a tool clips length, cut from the body, never the disclosures.

## Script (read as a single first-person analyst monologue)

> **[OPENING DISCLOSURE, keep verbatim]**
> Quick note before I start. This is an AI-generated voice. Nothing you are about
> to hear was spoken by a real person.

I looked at Syracuse Women's Lacrosse's 2025 season to answer one question. If
you want two more wins next year, do you fix the offense or the defense?

The data points clearly at offense. In the ten games Syracuse won, they scored
about fifteen and a half goals a game. In the nine they lost, that dropped to
under nine. The defense slipped too, but far less: from about ten goals allowed
in wins to thirteen in losses. So the swing on offense was more than twice the
swing on defense.

The clearest tell is shooting. Syracuse shot forty-nine percent in wins, and
just thirty-seven percent in losses. They were not getting buried on defense.
They were going cold and failing to finish.

And these games were winnable. Three of the nine losses were by a single goal.
You do not need a roster overhaul. You need a few more shots to go in.

If I had to pick one player to build around, it is Emma Ward. She led the team
in points and in assists, and she played every game. She is the engine. Sharpen
her finishing, and the whole offense lifts with her.

Focus on offense. The margin between this season and a better one is finishing.

> **[CLOSING DISCLOSURE, keep verbatim]**
> This was a synthetic, AI-generated voice, created for a research assignment.

## Delivery notes for TTS / video tools
- Punctuation is deliberate: short sentences and periods (not commas) at the
 data beats, so the engine inserts pauses where a human analyst would breathe.
- Numbers are spelled the way they should be spoken ("forty-nine percent"), which
 avoids the common TTS failure of reading "49%" as "forty nine percent sign" or
 rushing the figure.
- Emma Ward is a real player named in a public stats table. This script uses her
 name in an **analytical** sense (citing published season stats); it does **not**
 impersonate her voice or likeness. The synthetic element is a **generic or
 self** voice/face reading analysis, which is within the task's rules.
